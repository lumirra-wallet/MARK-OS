## However, one important point

You said:

> "I want an agent that will do research on the internet to learn new skills and knowledge and wisdom daily."

I think that's a great long-term feature, but **it shouldn't browse and permanently "teach itself" without your review**. Websites can be wrong, outdated, or even malicious.

Instead, I'd design it like this:

1. MARK searches trusted sources.
2. MARK summarizes what it found.
3. MARK presents it to **Mr. Smart**.
4. You approve what should become part of its long-term knowledge base.
5. MARK stores the approved information in your Obsidian vault or another knowledge store.

That keeps the system reliable while still allowing it to continuously discover new information.
