Milestone 6 – MARK Mind OS v1

PROJECT

Project Name: SmartAgent

Primary AI: MARK

Owner: Mr. Smart

Current Version: v0.6

MISSION

Transform MARK from a tool-based AI assistant into a computational digital organism with a persistent internal mind.

This milestone DOES NOT introduce consciousness.

Instead, it introduces computational self-awareness through a continuously maintained Self Model, Executive Controller, Working Memory, Attention System, Reflection System, and Homeostasis Engine.

MARK must continuously maintain an internal representation of itself and its current state.

=========================================================
1. EXECUTIVE CONTROLLER
=========================================================

This becomes MARK's central coordinator.

Responsibilities:

• Current Goal
• Current Task
• Current Mission
• Active Skills
• Active Tools
• Active Model
• Running Processes
• Priority Queue
• Interrupt Handling
• Decision Coordination
• Task Scheduling
• Health Monitoring

The Executive Controller is the only component allowed to coordinate all major systems.

=========================================================
2. SELF MODEL
=========================================================

Create a continuously updated internal representation of MARK.

The Self Model must answer:

Who am I?

Who owns me?

What is my mission?

What am I doing?

What tools do I have?

What skills do I know?

What model am I using?

What is my confidence?

How healthy am I?

What changed recently?

What am I currently learning?

What goals am I pursuing?

What should I do next?

Store this as a dynamic object rather than reading files every time.
