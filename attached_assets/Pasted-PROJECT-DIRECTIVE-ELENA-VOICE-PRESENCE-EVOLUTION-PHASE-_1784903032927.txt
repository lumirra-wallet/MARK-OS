PROJECT DIRECTIVE — ELENA VOICE & PRESENCE EVOLUTION (PHASE 1)
Mission

Transform Elena from a chatbot into a persistent AI companion.

The goal is not to add features.

The goal is to make Elena behave like a real conversational assistant similar to JARVIS.

Conversation must always feel like speaking to the same intelligent person.

Everything below is mandatory.

Core Philosophy

Elena is not an AI model.

Elena is the operating system.

The NVIDIA reasoning model is only one component of Elena's brain.

Every interaction must pass through Elena first.

Objective 12 — Success Criteria

Success is not measured by feature count.

Success is measured by one question.

"When I talk to Elena tomorrow,

does she feel more alive than she did today?"

Every engineering decision should improve that feeling.

If a feature does not contribute to presence,

conversation,

memory,

or intelligence,

do not build it.

IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION RULES
Do not replace existing systems unless necessary.
Implement incrementally with feature flags where appropriate.
Preserve compatibility with the current voice pipeline.
Document every architectural decision.
Do not fake behavior or memories.
Report any technical limitations honestly.

Expected outcome: Elena should increasingly resemble a persistent, emotionally aware AI companion similar in conversational feel to JARVIS, while remaining grounded in real memory, real reasoning, and verifiable knowledge rather than simulated personality.
