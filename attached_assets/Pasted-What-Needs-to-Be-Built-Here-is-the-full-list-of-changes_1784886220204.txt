The One Big Architectural Truth
MARK's pipeline is: Speech → Text → Thought → Speech (STT → LLM → TTS). This chain has latency at every step. ChatGPT Voice mode collapses this into Speech → Speech (audio directly in, audio directly out). That requires a different kind of model that doesn't exist in a free local setup.

What we CAN do is make the chain feel faster by:

Starting something immediately (the "thinking" cue)
Making Kokoro start sooner (smaller first sentence, pre-warmed model)
Making the LLM call start sooner (speculative dispatch on partial)
Removing the visual "typing" experience and making it feel like a call
