---

I also recommend one architectural improvement beyond this: don't build MARK as "an Ollama app" or "a GitHub Models app." Build it as a provider-agnostic AI operating system. Every AI feature—chat, agents, RAG, memory, voice, planning, coding, and evaluation—should go through the same provider interface. Then switching between GitHub Models during development and local Ollama later becomes a one-click change instead of a major refactor. That design will make future additions like OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or local vLLM much easier.
